## Platform

web

## Users

Three equally weighted audiences, each with their own flows and dashboards:

- **Customers** — browse the catalog (t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, tote bags, notebooks, posters, phone cases, stickers), customize or pick existing designs, and order prints for themselves or an occasion.
- **Designers** — upload artwork once and earn from every unit sold, without holding inventory or printing equipment. They track sales and earnings from a dedicated dashboard.
- **Print shops** — receive ready-to-execute orders scoped to the products they support, complete with print files and shipping data, and fulfill them from their own dashboard.

An admin role also exists (users, orders, shipping, earnings/payments, reports) to operate the marketplace across all three sides.

## Product Purpose

PalPrints is a Palestinian print-on-demand marketplace that brings designers, print shops, and customers together in one place, with delivery partnerships handling the last mile. It exists so a designer can sell physical products without inventory or equipment, a print shop can fill capacity with ready-to-run jobs, and a customer can get a custom or existing design printed and delivered.

## Positioning

A local/Arabic-market print-on-demand platform, not a global generalist like Printful or Redbubble: it connects designers directly with local print shops and delivery partners inside the Palestinian/Arabic market, in Arabic (RTL) end to end.

## Operating Context

- Multi-page static site (no build framework detected), Arabic-language, RTL layout throughout.
- Product catalog pages per category (t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, stickers, paper printing, etc.) plus a design studio and a guided "choose a product" flow.
- Role-based registration (customer / designer / print shop) from a single sign-up form.
- Separate dashboards for designer, print shop, and admin; customer-facing profile, favorites, orders/basket, and checkout flows.
- A homepage "how it works" video and partner sections pitch the designer and print-shop sides directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Designers: upload designs, get them applied across multiple product types, see acceptance notifications and a live sales/earnings dashboard, earn per unit sold — no inventory or printing equipment required on their end.
- Print shops: receive only orders for the products they support, each with print-ready files and shipping/customer data; fulfillment/turnaround is tracked (e.g. shipping management, earnings/payments).
- Admin: manages users, orders, shipping, print/design management, earnings & payments, reports, and support across the marketplace.
- Language/direction: Arabic and RTL are the only confirmed locale/direction today; no i18n for other languages is established.

## Product Principles

1. Local-first: decisions should fit the Palestinian/Arabic market and RTL Arabic UX, not default to global-generic print-on-demand conventions.
2. Three-sided equality: customer, designer, and print-shop experiences are all primary — no side is a secondary afterthought.
3. Zero-risk creation for designers: the product promise is design-once, earn-repeatedly, with no inventory or equipment burden shifted to them.
4. Operational precision for print shops: only relevant, ready-to-execute orders with complete print and shipping data — never generic queues.
